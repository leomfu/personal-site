### How I learn

I'm not a programmer. At first I couldn't even tell you whether a "large language model" and ChatGPT were the same thing. So step one wasn't learning a framework — it was building myself a knowledge base: Obsidian plus Claude Code, digesting every piece of material I read into notes that link to each other.

What makes this setup worth it is that what I learn doesn't get forgotten the moment I'm done with it — it stacks up, layer by layer. There's a rules file in the knowledge base that I wrote myself, defining how material comes in, how it gets looked up, and how the whole thing gets a regular checkup — and the checkup is all about hunting down conclusions that contradict each other.
